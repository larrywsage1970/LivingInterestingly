Emergency alerts

Issued alerts only: weather (NWS); county, state and national emergency alerts sent through IPAWS (including Presidential alerts); and DHS national terrorism advisories.

    Disasters & health

    Air quality (EPA AirNow), earthquakes within 300 km in the last 7 days (USGS), events at nearby nuclear plants (NRC: Perry, Beaver Valley, Davis-Besse), FEMA disaster declarations for this county, and CDC U.S. outbreak notices from the last 60 days.

      Power

      Grid threats: geomagnetic storms that can damage the power grid (NOAA, G4 severe and G5 extreme matter), and federal cyber advisories about attacks on critical infrastructure (CISA, last 30 days).

        National

        FEMA disaster and emergency declarations anywhere in the U.S. in the last 7 days. Context only; this does not change the banner. National alerts (Presidential, DHS) appear under Emergency alerts.

          Forecast

          Now, plus any period in the next 48 hours with storms, snow, ice or a hard freeze. Forecasts are not events.

              Ask

              Answers come only from the primary sources on this board, with each source and time cited. Not for emergencies: call 911.

              Before you believe a post

              1. Who said it? Name the primary source (NWS, county or state EMA, FEMA, PJM, FirstEnergy). No source, no belief.
              2. Event or risk? Has an alert actually been issued, or is it a forecast, an assessment or someone's opinion?
              3. Where and when? Does it name your county and give a date and time, or is it vague and undated?
              4. Can you find it? Open the source page yourself. If it isn't there, treat the post as unverified.
